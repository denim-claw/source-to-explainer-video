# Visual routing — choose the depiction before creating assets

## Minimal scene preflight

Record five fields before building assets:
1. audience, assumed knowledge and one learning target;
2. source claim and essential conditions;
3. visual family: human situation, computation, physical process or abstract relation;
4. visible action/change and what it explains;
5. reusable assets and a few scene-essential additions.

Choose what the viewer needs to see, not what a stock asset kit happens to contain.
Preserve the approved mode and renderer. Use 3D only when approved and when depth,
staging or physical arrangement adds information; 2D illustrations need not be diagrams.

## Route by claim, not whole-book genre

- **Human situation:** recognizable people, places, decisions, reactions and consequences.
  In an approved 2D film, use flat people/city/object illustrations when concrete actors
  explain the claim; do not replace them with anonymous labelled boxes or balls. A room
  or soldier background is not explanatory if all the meaning remains in narration.
- **Computation:** tokens, layer stacks, matrices, queues, cache slots and tool-call paths.
  Preserve execution order and causal direction; attention links are not token motion.
  Avoid decorative robots/server rooms unless the source actually needs them.
- **Physical process:** relevant spatial relationships, force directions, reference frames,
  scale and mechanisms. Label time compression and nonphysical scale. Attractive motion
  is not a scientifically verified simulation.
- **Abstract relation:** concise direct-label flows, classifications, comparisons, feedback
  and decision trees. Do not stage every abstraction as character acting or force every
  concrete situation into a generic box-arrow template.

Match detail to the viewer. A beginner may need a concrete situation before abstraction;
classification can start with a taxonomy. Keep actors, colors and roles consistent when
linking an illustration to a diagram. Animate the explanatory relationship, not idle wobble.
Declare invented staging/metaphors rather than attributing them to the source.

## Educational fit and evidence boundaries

- Identify whether the target is recognition, causal explanation, condition comparison
  or application to a new case. Each picture needs a job supporting that target.
- Consider situation → visible change → compact abstraction for unfamiliar cases. This
  is a design choice with domain limits, not a universally proven history-teaching method.
- Synchronize meaningful changes with speech; place short labels near their objects.
  Exclude unrelated detail without mechanically removing useful accessibility captions.
- Compare a bounded representative same-content narrated treatment before scaling a
  redesign. If a complete chapter is explicitly requested for review, deliver that whole
  chapter instead of substituting an excerpt, and hold the rest of the series.
- Keep source claim, narration, original audio, duration and essential information fixed;
  document unavoidable differences. Use a causal explanation/new-case question as well
  as preference. One user's response is not a controlled learning-effect experiment.
- Report playback/readability, source accuracy, design rationale and learner performance
  separately. More illustration, animation or 3D does not guarantee comprehension.
  Without a learning test, educational effectiveness remains an informed hypothesis.

Research anchors (retrieve and inspect the source before citing):
- Mayer, coherence/signaling/contiguity principles:
  https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108894333.019
- Fyfe et al., concreteness-fading systematic review:
  https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1036777 — primarily mathematics/science; transfer to other
  domains is design inference, not directly demonstrated domain evidence.
- Höffler & Leutner, instructional-animation meta-analysis:
  https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ780451 — benefits depend on instructional content and
  representation; not evidence that animation always wins or 3D is superior to 2D.

## Implementation honesty

Build only assets needed by an approved scene, test them, and record provenance/rights.
A listed asset family is not proof that polished characters, clips or specialist diagrams
exist. Inspect the actual bundled kit and its notices before reuse. Distinguish creation
method (procedural Pillow, authored SVG, image generation) from visual style and renderer;
see `production-discipline.md`. A clean frame and valid playback do not prove the source
argument or learning target was conveyed.
